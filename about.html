A thoughtful home setting

More connection.
More peace of mind.

T.L.C. Footprints is a licensed family home daycare in Elk Grove built for the early years that matter so much. We keep our days calm, warm, and predictable—so children know what to expect and parents can head into the day with confidence.

From the first hello to the end-of-day handoff, we make space for the small details: a favorite comfort item, a new skill to celebrate, a gentle transition, or a parent’s question. Those everyday moments are where trust is built.
