Parent FAQs

Clear answers before
your first visit.

We’ll always welcome the questions specific to your child. These are a few families ask first.

Ask a question →
What ages do you accept?−

We welcome children from 1 month through 5 years old, with care tailored to each child’s stage and daily rhythm.

What are your hours?+

Care is offered Monday through Friday, from 7:30 AM to 6:00 PM.

Do you accept Child Action?+

Yes. Child Action is accepted. Please reach out so we can help with the next steps.

How does availability work?+

Openings change as children grow and schedules shift. We’ll confirm current options based on your child’s age, desired start date, and schedule.

What should I bring for my child?+

Before your child begins, you’ll receive a personalized list based on age, routine, feeding needs, and comfort items.

What happens if my child is sick or needs medication?+

Current illness, exclusion, medication, and emergency procedures are reviewed with every family before enrollment.
