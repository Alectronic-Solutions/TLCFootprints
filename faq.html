These answers cover the questions families commonly ask first.

Ask a question →
What ages do you accept?−

We welcome children from 1 month through 5 years old. We’ll discuss your child’s needs and current availability together.

What are your hours?+

Care is offered Monday through Friday, from 7:30 AM to 6:00 PM.

Do you accept Child Action?+

Yes. Child Action is accepted. Please reach out so we can help you understand the next steps.

How do I schedule a tour?+

Use the tour-request form to share your needs. Once online delivery is live, we’ll respond to arrange a time to visit, meet, and ask questions.

How does availability work?+

Openings change as children grow and schedules shift. We’ll confirm the current options based on your child’s age, desired start date, and care schedule.

What should I bring for my child?+

We’ll provide a personalized list during enrollment based on your child’s age, daily routine, feeding needs, and comfort items.

How do you handle meals, rest, and allergies?+

We’ll talk through your child’s current routine, food needs, allergies, and rest needs before enrollment so expectations are clear from the beginning.

What happens if my child is sick or needs medication?+

Current illness, exclusion, medication, and emergency procedures are reviewed with every family before enrollment. Please ask for the most current policy during your tour.

How do you communicate with parents?+

We believe communication builds confidence. We’ll agree on an update rhythm that works for your family and talk through the best way to share important information.

Does a tour request guarantee enrollment?+

No. A tour or inquiry simply starts a conversation. Enrollment depends on available space, current policies, and whether the setting feels like the right fit for everyone.
